About Us

Built by Neighbors, Run by Neighbors

Three Neighbors Handyman Group started with a simple belief: the best person to call when something needs fixing is someone who lives right here in your community.

We're a local handyman team based in Independence, KY, serving homeowners and businesses across Northern Kentucky. From a fresh coat of paint to a full fence installation, we treat every job like it's our own home.

We're fully insured, dependable, and proud of the work we leave behind. Whether it's a quick fix or a big project — we show up, we communicate, and we get it done right.
