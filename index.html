About Us

We're Your Neighbors

Three Neighbors Handyman Group was built on a simple idea: your neighbors should be the first people you call when something needs fixing.

Jeff and Randy are local tradesmen based in Independence, KY, serving homeowners and businesses across Northern Kentucky. From a fresh coat of paint to a full fence installation, we treat every job like it's our own home.

We're fully insured, dependable, and proud of the work we leave behind. Whether it's a quick fix or a big project — we show up, we communicate, and we get it done right.
